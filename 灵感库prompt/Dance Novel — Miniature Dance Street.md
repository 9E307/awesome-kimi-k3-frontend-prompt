Dance Novel — Miniature Dance Street

## 提示词
### Dance Novel — Prompt

Create **“Dance Novel”**, an interactive 3D music street where users choose a miniature character and join an ongoing dance party. Deliver both a **playable website** and an **MP4 video with synchronized music**.

**ART DIRECTION**

Stylized 3D miniature diorama inspired by the retro diner dance scene in *Pulp Fiction*. A cinematic palette of deep burgundy, cherry red, warm mustard yellow, creamy ivory, near-black charcoal, and restrained turquoise neon accents. Warm amber spotlights, intimate pools of light, soft shadows, subtle atmospheric glow, and matte painted surfaces.

A fictional nighttime entertainment block with a retro diner, record shop, small bar, glowing shop windows, vintage signage, and a central checkerboard dance floor. Low-poly architecture, clean geometric shapes, handcrafted miniature details, slightly exaggerated proportions, and a carefully composed three-quarter isometric view. The whole street should feel like a collectible film set: compact, stylish, playful, and alive.

Use original characters and fictional storefronts. Express the cinematic influence through color, lighting, costume, and mood.

**CHARACTERS AND DANCING**

Small stylized toy-like dancers with oversized heads, simplified faces, expressive poses, and distinct silhouettes. Include a character in a black suit and white shirt, a character in a white blouse and black trousers, a mustard-yellow outfit, and a burgundy outfit.

Create smooth, looping retro twist and swing-inspired dance animations: alternating foot pivots, side steps, loose arm swings, playful hand gestures, and gentle head bobs. Keep feet grounded and movement rhythmically convincing. Give each character a different dance personality while keeping everyone on the same beat.

**INTERACTIVE WEBSITE**

Open with the street already dancing, accompanied by a clear **“Choose your dancer”** invitation. Let users preview characters on a small rotating platform, select one, and press **“Join the dance.”** Start the music after the user’s first interaction.

The selected character walks onto an available dance-floor spot and seamlessly joins the choreography. Highlight the user’s dancer with a subtle warm spotlight. Allow users to change dancers, switch between three dance moves, rotate and zoom the scene, and toggle music.

Make the neighborhood respond to the beat: streetlights pulse gently, record-shop displays rotate, window lights alternate, and small rooftop elements bounce subtly. Keep the dancers as the visual focus.

Include a **“Record my dance”** action that exports a short MP4 featuring the selected character and current scene, with music included.

**MUSIC**

Use an upbeat instrumental retro dance track with a twist, swing, or early rock-and-roll feel. Both the composition and the specific recording must be verified as public domain for the intended distribution territories; alternatively, use a verified CC0 track. Do not assume that an old composition makes a modern recording public domain. Provide the source and rights information with the deliverables.

**MP4 OUTPUT**

Deliver a polished 20–30-second, 1080p MP4 with synchronized audio: begin with an establishing view of the miniature street, move closer to the featured dancer, then pull back to reveal the whole neighborhood dancing. Use smooth cinematic camera movement and a clean ending suitable for replay.

**DON’T INCLUDE**

Photorealistic people, celebrity likenesses, copied film footage or dialogue, the original film soundtrack, unverified music rights, realistic violence, weapons, excessive cyberpunk neon, crowded layouts, glossy plastic, broken anatomy, sliding feet, abrupt animation loops, aggressive flashing, confusing controls, watermarks, or placeholder export buttons.
